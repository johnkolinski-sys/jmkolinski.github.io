Openings

EMSI is currently reviewing applications at the PhD level for two positions.

1 · PhD — Crack front stability in 3D

A student studying fracture, with a prospective thesis on crack-front stability in 3D.

What we seek

A student with substantial laboratory experience and familiarity with general laboratory instrumentation. Experience with microscopy or imaging is a plus but not required. The qualified applicant will have some experience with continuum solid mechanics. We seek applicants with a master's degree at the desired start date, but will consider applicants with a bachelor's degree.

What we offer

A science-first lab for investigating interfacial mechanical phenomena using all available tools and methods, with a hallmark of novel experimental approaches. You will be encouraged to achieve your potential as a scientist and have access to all the required tools. We have a vibrant, driven team with a high standard of work–life balance.

2 · PhD — Driven wetting and contact through air

A student studying contact formation, with a prospective thesis on driven wetting and contact through air.

What we seek

A student with substantial laboratory experience and familiarity with general laboratory instrumentation. Experience with microscopy or imaging is a plus but not required. The qualified applicant will have some experience with continuum fluid and solid mechanics. We seek applicants with a master's degree at the desired start date, but will consider applicants with a bachelor's degree.

What we offer

A science-first lab for investigating interfacial mechanical phenomena using all available tools and methods, with a hallmark of novel experimental approaches. You will be encouraged to achieve your potential as a scientist and have access to all the required tools. We have a vibrant, driven team with a high standard of work–life balance.

How to apply

Interested applicants are encouraged to send the following (PDF preferred) to Dr. John Kolinski at john.kolinski@epfl.ch:

1. A cover letter summarizing your qualifications and interest in joining the lab.
2. A CV including a list of published scientific work and a description of your scientific goals.

We are committed to hiring diversely and inclusively.

Student internships

If you wish to come as an intern, apply to the E3 program and list me as a potential host. For internships as part of a degree program, contact me on a case-by-case basis.
