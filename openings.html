Openings

We are recruiting PhD students and postdocs to study the fracture of soft solids at IUSTI (CNRS / Aix-Marseille Université). Each call below links to a full description (PDF).

Hierarchy of Scales SNSF project · The Hierarchy of Scales in the Fracture of Soft and Slender Bodies (2025–2029) · with ETH Zurich (D. Kammer) and CNRS Lyon (M. Adda-Bedia)

PhD · Crack initiation in a slender body — WP 1.3

How does a crack begin in a soft, slender body?

Controlled nucleation experiments in hydrogels: from penny-shaped defects at internal inclusions to through-cracks, imaged in 3D by light-sheet microscopy, in collaboration with simulations at ETH Zurich and theory in Lyon.

3-year doctoral contract · experimental · start as soon as possible · Read the full call (PDF) →

PhD · The crack tip as a rheometer — WP 2.1

Measuring nonlinear elasticity in the near-tip fields of soft cracks.

Reconstruct the full displacement and strain fields near a propagating crack by tracking fluorescent tracers, measure the crack-tip opening displacement, and extract the material's nonlinear elastic response at failure-relevant strains.

3-year doctoral contract · experimental · start as soon as possible · Read the full call (PDF) →

PhD · From network architecture to fracture energy — WP 3.2

Linking polymer network structure to dissipation in soft fracture.

Compare near-ideal tetra-PEG networks with heterogeneous polyacrylamide gels, measuring fracture energy by pure-shear, J-integral, and crack-tip opening methods, with network structure imaged directly by cryo-electron microscopy.

3-year doctoral contract · experimental · start as soon as possible · Read the full call (PDF) →

Postdoc · Bridging the scales of soft fracture — WP 1–3

Experiments linking slenderness, nonlinear elasticity, and dissipation at the crack tip.

The experimental backbone of the project: develop the shared measurement methods, link the three PhD work packages, and serve as the main experimental interface with the simulation group at ETH Zurich and the theory group in Lyon.

Up to 3 years (project ends 2029) · experimental · start as soon as possible · Read the full call (PDF) →

Viscocrack SNSF project · Crack path prediction and control in nonlinearly viscoelastic materials (ends August 2028) · with UCLA (L. Jin), EPFL (E. Amstad), IRPHE (M.-J. Dalbe)

Postdoc · Crack path selection — Aim 3

How do nonlinearly viscoelastic cracks select their paths?

Measure mixed-mode crack path selection in thin-sheet specimens as a function of mode mixity and loading rate, using high-speed imaging and digital image correlation, to calibrate and test phase-field models developed with the Jin group at UCLA.

18 months (project ends August 2028) · experimental · start as soon as possible · Read the full call (PDF) →

Postdoc · Steering cracks with light — Aim 4

Predicting and controlling crack paths in soft solids by digital light patterning.

Develop a digital-micromirror set-up to imprint cross-link-density landscapes in photo-curable soft solids, measure how they guide crack propagation, and pursue inverse design of crack paths with phase-field simulations.

18 months (project ends August 2028) · experimental · start as soon as possible · Read the full call (PDF) →
