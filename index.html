John M. Kolinski

Assistant Professor & Group Leader · Engineering Mechanics of Soft Interfaces (EMSI)

Institute of Mechanical Engineering, School of Engineering (STI), EPFL

My group investigates the mechanics of soft interfaces — how cracks propagate, how surfaces come into contact, and how droplets wet solids. We develop novel high-speed and interferometric imaging methods to observe these fast, singular processes directly, across spatial and temporal scales, and use the resulting kinematic data to test and extend the theory of interfacial mechanics.
